Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to fabiangerdes92@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my game saved?

On your device, inside the app. There is no account and no server copy. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, the save file is part of your iPhone backup and comes back when you restore the backup on a new device.

Can I move my reef to a new iPhone?

Yes, by restoring your iPhone from an iCloud or Finder backup. Reinstalling the app on the same device without a backup starts a new reef.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. The save file is removed with it. We never receive your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Notifications

Reminders are optional and scheduled on your device only. You can switch them off any time in iOS Settings › Notifications › Fish & Coral.
